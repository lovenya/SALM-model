Pair construction and cleaning

We start from AudioCaps training clips, captions and durations. Qwen3-32B proposes a concise, contextually plausible utterance and a rationale; XTTS-v2 synthesizes the speech. For dataset cleaning, Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct judges each pair with G-Eval Audio. We filter the two directions independently, removing a pair when its weighted score is 3 or lower, its scenario-plausibility score is 2 or lower, or its target-modality-success score is 1.

The rationale is saved dataset metadata that records the intended connection between the caption and utterance. It is not independent evidence that the pair is correct.
