Why these belong together

Qwen3-32B generated a short utterance and a brief rationale from the audio caption and duration. Speech was synthesized with XTTS-v2, then the pairs were filtered separately for each direction using G-Eval Audio. The rationale describes the intended context; it is generated metadata, not independent evidence that the pair is correct.

A consistent listening subset

100 common test IDs from the existing curated bundle, selected with seed 20260917. The full bundle balances speaker gender and includes varied emotions and scene groups.

This is a curated inspection subset, not a random estimate of overall quality. None of the headline results are calculated from these examples alone.
