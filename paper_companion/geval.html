MS and PQ are scored on the output alone. SP, CD and EF/AF use the input and output together. The Overall score averages the five criteria for each successfully scored output.

Does the judge notice a wrong pairing?

PAIRED CONTROLS

The output is a real ground-truth recording in both conditions. For the mismatched condition, it is paired with a different sample's input. The table compares 120 selected inputs in each direction to see whether context-dependent criteria respond to the relationship between the recordings.

These are class-disjoint strong negatives drawn from clean-v2 test pairs, not an independent benchmark. Output-only criteria (MS and PQ) are omitted because changing the input should not change what they measure. Individual mismatches can still receive high scores; this v6 diagnostic is not a human-agreement guarantee.

What the five criteria ask

One score and written feedback per criterion

The judge receives two ordered clips for input-plus-output criteria, and only the generated clip for output-only criteria. Dataset captions, transcripts and rationales are not passed to this v6 judge.
